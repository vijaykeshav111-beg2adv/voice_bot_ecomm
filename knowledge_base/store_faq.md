# ShopMate Store FAQ

## Store overview
ShopMate is a demo online store with a sample catalog of electronics, fashion, and home products. Current product names, SKUs, prices, descriptions, and stock are shown by the live product catalog.

## Orders
An order is created only after the customer reviews the products, quantities, total, customer name, and delivery address and explicitly confirms. A successful order returns an order reference beginning with ORD-. Keep this reference to check the order status.

## Payments
This demo does not process real payments and does not collect payment-card details. Payment methods and a payment gateway are not configured in this demo.

## Shipping and delivery
The demo records the delivery address but is not connected to a courier. It does not provide live shipment tracking or verified delivery estimates. Order tracking shows the status recorded by the application.

## Sample return and refund policy
For this demo, a return may be requested within 7 calendar days after delivery. Items should be unused, undamaged, and returned with original packaging, accessories, and proof of purchase. Report a wrong or damaged item within 48 hours and include the order reference and clear photos. Personalized items, digital goods, and opened hygiene-sensitive items are excluded unless they arrived damaged or incorrect.

To request a return, email help@shopmate.demo with the order reference, reason, and relevant photos. After an approved return is received and inspected, a refund would typically be initiated within 5–7 business days. Actual posting time would depend on the payment provider. This demo does not process real payments or refunds. Customers may request cancellation before an order is marked dispatched; cancellation after dispatch is not guaranteed. The demo has no real courier or return-shipping service, so support would need to confirm the return method.

These are illustrative sample rules only. Replace them with the merchant's approved policy before production use.

## Privacy
The store should collect only the information needed to create an order. Customer order information should not be shared with other customers. Public production deployment requires appropriate authentication and HTTPS.
